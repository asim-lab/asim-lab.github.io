Research shaped by real-world systems and measurable impact.

I am a computer scientist and academic working across artificial intelligence, machine learning, software engineering, vehicular networks, and biomedical applications. My work connects rigorous computational methods with practical challenges in mobility, healthcare, energy, and geoscience.
